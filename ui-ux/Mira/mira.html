MIRA-04

UI/UX philosophy

Mira audits every design against these principles MIRA-05

Principles

  • Calm and credible — sustainability data earns trust through clarity, not decoration.
  • Guided, never clever — suppliers and managers are busy; every flow explains itself.
  • Accessibility is non-negotiable — WCAG 2.2 AA, color-blind-safe palettes, keyboard-first.
  • Reuse before redesign — compose from the design system; new patterns need a reason.
  • Honest states — drafts look like drafts; approved work is visibly locked.
