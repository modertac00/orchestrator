Drafted by Atlas from D-05 and D-06 · prompt template ATLAS-REQ-v2 · run #112

Problem statement

EcoTrace customers must report Scope 3 supplier emissions under CSRD, but 68% of their suppliers have no structured way to submit emissions data. Today it arrives by email and spreadsheet, is error-prone, and cannot be audited.

Goals & success metrics

  • Suppliers submit emissions data through a guided intake form — target: 80% of invited suppliers submit without support tickets.
  • Data is validated at entry — target: <5% of submissions rejected downstream.
  • Every value carries audit lineage (who, when, source document).

User stories

  • As a supplier, I can submit energy and fuel usage per reporting period so my customer's Scope 3 report includes my data.
  • As a sustainability manager, I can see which suppliers have submitted, been reminded, or are overdue.
  • As an auditor, I can trace every reported value back to its submission and submitter.

Out of scope

  • Automatic emissions estimation from spend data (Phase 2 candidate).
  • Supplier onboarding/contracting — handled by existing CRM flow.
