Summary GAIA-08

The EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) enters its second wave in 2026, extending mandatory ESRS-aligned reporting to large non-listed companies 1. Scope 3 supplier emissions disclosure moves from "comply or explain" to mandatory quantitative reporting for most in-scope companies 2, which directly increases demand for supplier data collection tooling like EcoTrace 4.

Key findings GAIA-09

  • Wave 2 applies to companies with 250+ employees or €50M+ turnover — an estimated 49,000 companies across the EU 1.
  • Scope 3 category 1 (purchased goods & services) is the largest reporting gap; 68% of surveyed companies lack supplier-level data 2 3.
  • Digital tagging (XBRL) of sustainability statements becomes mandatory, favoring products with structured data export 5.
  • Member-state transposition still varies; Germany and France add stricter assurance requirements 6.

Risks & opportunities GAIA-10

  • Opportunity: supplier emissions intake (REQ-031 candidate) matches the largest compliance gap 2.
  • Risk: assurance requirements may demand audit-grade data lineage — affects architecture early.
