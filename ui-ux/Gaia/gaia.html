Research question & objectives GAIA-08

RQ. Do supplier-level primary data programmes reduce Scope 3 category 1 estimation error compared with spend-based methods 3 7?

  • O1. Map estimation methods used in peer-reviewed Scope 3 studies, 2019–2026.
  • O2. Compare reported error ranges by method and by sample size 12.
  • O3. Identify the conditions under which primary data collection is cost-justified.

Findings GAIA-09

  • Across 38 included studies, supplier-primary methods report a mean estimation error of 9% against 42% for industry-average proxies 3 7 12.
  • Error falls sharply once primary coverage passes ~40% of purchased-goods spend; beyond ~70% the marginal gain is small 12.
  • Only 11 of 38 studies report an uncertainty interval at all — the field's largest reporting weakness 3.

Discussion · limitations · research gaps GAIA-10

  • Discussion: the error gap between primary and proxy methods is consistent across sectors, but 6 studies report no significant difference in low-complexity supply chains 5.
  • Limitation: English-language, peer-reviewed only; grey literature and vendor benchmarks excluded by protocol.
  • Research gap: no longitudinal study follows the same supplier base for more than two reporting cycles 7.
