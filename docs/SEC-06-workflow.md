# SEC-06 — Example End-to-End Workflow

One product initiative flowing through the ecosystem:

1. **Business & market research**
   AI compiles competitor matrix and trend brief → Product lead validates opportunity.
2. **Finance planning**
   AI drafts 12-month scenario (base / optimistic / conservative) → Finance lead adjusts assumptions.
3. **Business analytics**
   AI proposes KPIs and dashboard spec → Analytics owner aligns with data warehouse reality.
4. **Product spec & UI/UX**
   AI drafts PRD sections and low-fi flows → Designer refines UX and visual language.
5. **Development**
   AI scaffolds features, APIs, and docs, checks code quality and standards, and supports developers during implementation → Engineers implement, review, and merge.
6. **Test design**
   AI generates test cases and automation stubs → QA prioritizes by risk and signs release.
7. **Feedback loop**
   Production metrics and incidents feed back into knowledge layer for the next cycle.
