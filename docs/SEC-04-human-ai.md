# SEC-04 — Human vs AI: Decision Guide

## Use AI heavily when…

- Task is repetitive and has clear inputs/outputs
- AI can improve productivity, efficiency, and quality without removing human ownership
- Output can be reviewed in minutes (draft doc, code snippet, test list)
- Errors are low-cost and easy to catch

## Keep humans in control when…

- Creativity, brand voice, or novel product direction is required
- Legal, financial, or security commitments are made
- Customer-facing promises or pricing are set
- Data is sensitive or compliance applies (GDPR, SOC2, etc.)
- Team needs accountability — someone must own the decision

## Human + AI operating principle

We will use AI as much as possible to improve productivity, efficiency, and quality, including enforcing code quality, engineering standards, and better development support. But we will not remove people from the process. We will slowly automate tasks from humans to AI where it makes sense, while humans remain in the picture to do the human work: judgment, creativity, accountability, customer understanding, leadership, and final decision-making.
