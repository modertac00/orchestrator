# SEC-01 — Vision

**Goal:** Build a company-wide AI ecosystem where specialized AI tools support every major business function — from market research to code and test design — while humans stay in charge of strategy, quality, and creative decisions.

Think of AI not as "replacing teams" but as a machine layer that:

- Accelerates research, drafting, analysis, and implementation
- Follows templates, checklists, and company standards consistently
- Produces first drafts, options, and summaries for human review
- Learns from human corrections to become more accurate over time

Humans remain essential where outcomes are uncertain, brand-sensitive, legally binding, or require genuine creativity.
